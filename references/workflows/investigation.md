# Investigation Workflow Delta

Apply these additions to the core routing flow for diagnosis or research:

- define the question, evidence standard, and stopping condition;
- reduce the search space with logs, queries, reproduction, or static evidence;
- treat one unknown root cause as depth, not breadth;
- parallelize only independent hypotheses or evidence sources;
- distinguish fact, observation, inference, hypothesis, and unknown;
- preserve counterevidence and failed hypotheses in compact form;
- escalate when evidence cannot support the required confidence.
